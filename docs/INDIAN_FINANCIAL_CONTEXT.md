# Indian Financial Context & Literacy Guidelines

This document outlines the core concepts, regulatory boundaries, and cultural practices used in WealthGenie to serve Indian beginners.

---

## 1. Currency & Numbering Rules

- **Currency Unit**: Indian Rupee (`₹` / `INR`). Never use `$` or other foreign currency symbols.
- **Number Grouping**: Indian numbering grouping (`en-IN`):
  - Values below ₹1 Lakh: `₹500`, `₹1,000`, `₹75,000`.
  - Values in Lakhs: `₹1,00,000` (1 Lakh), `₹2,50,000` (2.5 Lakhs), `₹10,00,000` (10 Lakhs).
  - Values in Crores: `₹1,00,00,000` (1 Crore).

---

## 2. Core Indian Financial Instruments

1. **Savings Accounts & DICGC Insurance**:
   - Primary operational liquidity.
   - Bank deposits in India are insured by the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC), an RBI subsidiary, up to ₹5,00,000 per depositor per bank (principal + interest).

2. **Emergency Cushion & Liquid Mutual Funds**:
   - Benchmark: 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses.
   - Preferred vehicles: High-interest savings account, bank sweep-in Fixed Deposits, or Liquid Mutual Funds.

3. **Fixed Deposits (FD) & Recurring Deposits (RD)**:
   - Capital preservation and assured return instruments for short-to-medium horizons.
   - Interest is taxable per individual tax slabs.

4. **Mutual Funds & Systematic Investment Plans (SIP)**:
   - An SIP is a method of investing a fixed sum periodically (monthly or weekly) into a mutual fund scheme, encouraging disciplined rupee-cost averaging.
   - Categorized by SEBI into Equity, Debt, Hybrid, and Solution Oriented.
   - Broad index funds tracking the Nifty 50 or Sensex offer low expense ratios and instant market diversification.

5. **Public Provident Fund (PPF)**:
   - 15-year sovereign-backed long-term savings instrument with EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) tax status under Section 80C. Annual ceiling of ₹1,50,000.

6. **Employees Provident Fund (EPF) & Voluntary PF (VPF)**:
   - Mandatory retirement savings for salaried employees, backed by the EPFO.

7. **National Pension System (NPS)**:
   - Voluntary defined contribution pension system regulated by PFRDA.

8. **Digital Payment & UPI Safety**:
   - Universal digital rails (Unified Payments Interface) managed by NPCI.
   - Core literacy rule: **UPI PIN is entered only to debit/pay money, never to receive money or collect cashbacks.**

---

## 3. Regulatory Framework & Non-Advisory Boundaries

- **SEBI**: Regulates securities, stock exchanges, mutual funds, and Registered Investment Advisers (RIA).
- **RBI**: Regulates currency, banking institutions, and digital payment infrastructure.
- **Educational Scope**: WealthGenie is purely an educational platform. It does not provide personalized portfolio allocation, tax filing, or security recommendations.
