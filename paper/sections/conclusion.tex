\section{Conclusion}

WealthGenie presents an integrated, SDG-aligned financial literacy and investment decision support platform for Indian beginners. The platform connects five core modules into a unified learning journey: Financial Literacy through the Learn module, Financial Assessment through the Assessment module, Risk-Aware Recommendations through the recommendation engine, Simulated Investment Learning through the Paper Trading module, and Financial Scenario Comparison through the Digital Twin module.

A user progresses from understanding financial concepts to assessing their personal financial position, exploring suitable investments informed by risk compatibility and verified data, experimenting with simulated trades, and comparing hypothetical scenarios against their baseline. Each module consumes the output of the preceding one, and the recommendation engine's deterministic, explainable methodology ensures that every suggestion is traceable to verified AMFI and SEBI dataset features or the user's own profile.

The platform's design aligns with SDG 4 through structured, source-checked financial literacy content; with SDG 8 through transparent household financial assessment; with SDG 10 through accessible, no-minimum educational guidance; and with SDG 12 through risk-aware, cost-conscious and simulation-supported decision making. The platform is positioned as an educational tool that supports these objectives, without claiming to measure or achieve SDG targets directly.

Implementation-consistency and explainability evidence for the deterministic assessment and Digital Twin components was obtained across synthetic profiles, and the recommendation engine was validated through 448 automated tests and a measured evaluation run with zero constraint violations. The platform is implemented as a React frontend, Node.js and Express backend, Supabase and PostgreSQL persistence, and Python dataset pipelines.

WealthGenie contributes to accessible financial education in the Indian context by combining verified data, transparent methodology and experiential learning within a single, coordinated platform.
